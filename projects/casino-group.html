Casino Group Financial Analysis

Financial Statement Analysis • Corporate Finance

Comprehensive financial statement analysis of Casino Group, evaluating profitability, liquidity, leverage and operational performance to assess the company's financial health and recommend strategic improvements.

Domain Financial Analysis
Tools Excel
Techniques Ratio Analysis
Year 2025

Project Snapshot

Objective

Evaluate Casino Group's financial performance following its restructuring by analysing its financial statements, key ratios, business strategy and future outlook.

Business Challenge

Despite reducing debt through restructuring and asset disposals, Casino continued to face weak profitability, liquidity pressure and a highly leveraged capital structure.

Key Conclusion

Casino is operationally improving but remains financially fragile. Continued deleveraging, tighter cost control and successful execution of its asset-light strategy are essential for sustainable recovery.

Project Details

Company Casino Group
Industry Retail
Tools Excel, PowerPoint
Analysis Financial Statement Analysis
Skills Ratio Analysis, Forecasting, Strategic Analysis, Corporate Finance
01

Financial Performance

Casino Group experienced a significant decline in operating performance between 2022 and 2024 as the company underwent major restructuring. Inflationary pressures, declining store traffic and restructuring costs negatively impacted EBITDA and EBIT margins.

Financial forecasts indicate gradual recovery as Casino continues its transformation towards an asset-light business model focused on franchising, operational efficiency and higher-margin retail formats.

02

Risk Analysis

Although restructuring significantly reduced debt, Casino continues to face elevated financial risk due to weak equity, high leverage and pressure on profitability.

The analysis highlights that sustainable recovery depends on strengthening the balance sheet while improving operational performance over the coming years.

03

Liquidity & Cash Flow

Working capital management showed signs of improvement after restructuring. However, liquidity remained constrained, highlighting the importance of maintaining efficient cash conversion and disciplined cost control.

Improvements in supplier financing and inventory management will remain critical for supporting future growth.

04

Strategic Recommendations

The project concludes that Casino's long-term recovery depends on continued deleveraging, tighter cost management, expansion of its franchise model and accelerated digital transformation.

Successful execution of these strategic initiatives would strengthen profitability, improve financial resilience and enhance long-term shareholder value.
